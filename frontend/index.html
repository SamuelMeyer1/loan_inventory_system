Hantera meddelanden

                  
Ändra standardtexter för SMTP-påminnelser och Teams-notiser utan att gå in i koden.

                

              

              

                

                  
Teams: nytt jobb

                  
                  
                  Variabler: {request_id}, {user_name}, {object_name}, {object_id}, {quantity}, {lending_date}, {return_date}
                

                

                  
SMTP: klar att hämta

                  
                  
                  Variabler: {request_id}, {user_name}, {object_name}, {object_id}, {quantity}, {lending_date}, {return_date}
                

                

                  
Teams: återlämnat lån

                  
                  
                  Variabler: {request_id}, {user_name}, {object_name}, {object_id}, {quantity}
                

                

                  
Teams: lagerlarm

                  
                  
                  Variabler: {object_name}, {object_id}, {level}, {available}, {min_quantity}
                

                

                  
SMTP: påminnelse

                  
                  
                  
                  Variabler: {loan_id}, {user_name}, {object_name}, {object_id}, {quantity}, {due_time}
